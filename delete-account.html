Delete Your Chinese SD Account

Chinese SD users can request deletion of their account and associated data by following the steps below.

How to request account deletion

  1. Send an email to chinesesd01@gmail.com.
  2. Use the subject: Delete Chinese SD Account.
  3. In the message, include the email address used for your Chinese SD account and state that you want the account deleted.
  4. We may ask you to verify ownership of the account before deletion.

Data that will be deleted

After verification, we will delete the Chinese SD account information and associated data under our control that is linked to that account, such as profile information and account-linked learning data, where applicable.

Data stored on your device

Some learning progress, settings, favourites, bookmarks, and profile information may be stored locally on your device. You can remove local app data by clearing Chinese SD app storage or uninstalling the app.

Data that may be retained

Limited records may be retained when required for security, fraud prevention, legal compliance, or to complete the deletion process. Information processed independently by third-party service providers may be subject to their own retention policies.
