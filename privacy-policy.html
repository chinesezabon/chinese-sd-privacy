Privacy Policy for Chinese SD

Last updated: 24 September 2026

Chinese SD ("the App", "we", "us", or "our") is an educational Android application for learning Chinese. This Privacy Policy explains what information the App accesses, how it is used, when it may be sent to service providers, and the choices available to users.

Developer: Chinese SD
Package name: com.chinesesd.app
Privacy contact: chinesesd01@gmail.com

1. Information the App may access or process

  • Microphone audio: used only when you start pronunciation or speaking features.
  • Speech recognition data: recorded speech may be sent to Google Cloud Speech-to-Text to convert speech into text and provide pronunciation-related functionality.
  • AI tutor content: questions and messages you submit to the AI tutor, together with limited learning context such as selected HSK level, language preference, and relevant learning progress, may be sent to Google's Gemini service to generate a response.
  • Profile information you choose to enter: for example a display name, target HSK level, learning progress, favorites, bookmarks, and related study settings.
  • Profile image: if you choose an image from your device for your profile, the App accesses the selected image for that purpose.
  • Technical/network information: third-party Google services used by the App may process ordinary technical information needed to provide, secure, and protect their services, such as network request information, service diagnostics, or app/device integrity signals where applicable.

2. How information is used

  • provide Chinese vocabulary, listening, writing, speaking, and pronunciation features;
  • recognize speech and evaluate speaking practice;
  • provide AI tutor responses and personalized learning assistance;
  • save your local learning progress, settings, favorites, bookmarks, and profile choices;
  • maintain security, prevent abuse, and troubleshoot technical issues.

3. Where information is stored

Learning progress, settings, favorites, bookmarks, and a profile image you select are primarily stored locally on your device by the App. The App currently does not require users to create a Chinese SD account in order to use its core features.

Information sent to Google Cloud Speech-to-Text or Google Gemini is processed by Google as a service provider for the relevant feature. The handling and retention of information by Google is also subject to Google's applicable privacy terms and service policies.

4. Sharing of information

We do not sell your personal information. We do not share personal information with advertisers. Information is sent to third-party service providers only when needed to provide a feature you use, such as Google Cloud Speech-to-Text, Google Gemini / Google AI services, and Google/Firebase security services where enabled.

5. Microphone and audio

The App requests microphone permission for speaking and pronunciation practice. Recording begins only after you initiate a feature that needs the microphone. Audio is used for the requested speaking or speech-recognition feature. The App does not use the microphone for advertising.

6. Photos and media

If you choose to set a profile image, the App may request access needed to select an image from your device. The App uses the image you select for your profile. It does not scan your photo library for advertising purposes.

7. Advertising

The current version of Chinese SD does not include third-party advertising SDKs and does not use personal information for targeted advertising.

8. Security

We use reasonable technical measures to protect information handled by the App. Network requests to supported Google cloud services are sent over encrypted HTTPS connections. No method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

9. Data retention and deletion

Data stored locally by Chinese SD generally remains on your device until you remove it, clear the App's storage, reset the relevant feature, or uninstall the App. AI chat information kept only in the current app session may be cleared when the session is cleared or the App process ends.

Information processed by third-party Google services may be retained according to the applicable Google service terms and policies. If you have a privacy or deletion request concerning information under our control, contact us at chinesesd01@gmail.com.

10. Children's privacy

Chinese SD is an educational application. We do not provide a user account registration flow that asks children to submit profile identity information to us. If a child uses features involving microphone audio or cloud-based AI/speech services, a parent or guardian should review this Privacy Policy and supervise use as required by applicable law.

11. International processing

Cloud service providers may process information on servers located outside your country. When you use cloud-based speech or AI features, information may therefore be processed in other jurisdictions in accordance with the provider's applicable privacy and security practices.

12. Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the App's features, data practices, or legal requirements change. The updated policy will show a revised "Last updated" date.
